OUR STORY

Building Kenya's
Digital Future.

Prime Web Solutions was founded with one mission: to make professional, high-quality web design accessible to Kenyan businesses of all sizes. We've seen too many local businesses lose opportunities simply because their online presence doesn't reflect the quality of their work.

We're a Karen-based team of designers and developers who care deeply about craft. We don't just build websites — we build digital tools that actually help businesses grow, generate leads, and serve customers better.

From small startups to established companies, we've delivered projects across e-commerce, SACCO management, interior design, electronics retail, and more. Every project gets our full attention, from the first call to the final launch.
